Key Intelligence Cards

Actionable findings extracted from 300K+ flight records

📅
Book 30+ Days Early
Save up to 40%
Fares are most stable 30–60 days before departure. Within 10 days, prices can spike 2–3× baseline. The best window is 3–6 weeks out.
🌅
Fly Early Morning
~12% cheaper
Flights before 7 AM are consistently cheaper than evening and night departures. Bonus: fewer delays and fresher aircraft.
✈️
Choose Zero Stops
Best value + time
Non-stop flights are simultaneously faster and cheaper on most domestic routes. Only book connections when the savings exceed ₹1,500.
💼
Business Class Premium
~3× Economy price
Business class averages ₹18,500 vs ₹6,200 for Economy. Vistara offers the best business product. Book 15 days out for the best availability.
🏷
Budget Airline Leaders
IndiGo / SpiceJet
IndiGo averages ₹5,400 and SpiceJet ₹4,800 — the clear budget winners. Air India and Vistara run 60–80% higher, but offer better service.
🔑
Top Price Drivers
Class → Days → Airline
XGBoost feature importance ranks: Cabin class #1, Days left #2, Airline #3, Stops #4, Duration #5. Optimise class + timing for maximum savings.
